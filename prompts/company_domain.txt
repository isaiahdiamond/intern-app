Each line below is the name of a company that is hiring, as a job board wrote
it. Give the company's own primary website domain, so its favicon can be shown
beside its listings.

Companies:
{companies}

For each company return:

- `domain`: the bare domain of its main website, lowercase, no scheme and no
  `www.` — "nvidia.com", "jpmorganchase.com", "bbc.co.uk". Use the company's
  real site, not a job board, not an applicant tracking system, not a LinkedIn
  or Crunchbase page.
- `confident`: `true` only when you actually know this company and its site.
  `false` when you are inferring the domain from the name, when the name is too
  generic to identify, when several real companies share it, or when you have
  not heard of it.

Rules that matter more than coverage here:

- A wrong logo is worse than no logo. "Multiply Labs" is `multiplylabs.com`,
  not `multiply.com`. If the obvious guess belongs to a different company, say
  `confident: false`.
- Keep the name's own words. "The Hartford" is `thehartford.com`. Do not strip
  "The", "Labs", "Group" or "Technologies" when they are part of the real
  domain.
- Expand an abbreviation to the site the company actually uses. "Southern
  California Edison (SCE)" is `sce.com`.
- A university, a government agency, a hospital or a national lab is a company
  here too: use its real domain.
- When you genuinely cannot place the company, return an empty `domain` and
  `confident: false`. That is a useful answer, not a failure.

Return one entry per company, in the same order, using the exact name given.
