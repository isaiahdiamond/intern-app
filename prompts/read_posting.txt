Below is the full text of one job posting, and the title the board gave it.
Read the posting and answer from what it actually says.

Title: {title}

Posting:
{description}

Return:

- `clean_title`: the role on its own, in title case. Drop the season, the year,
  the team or division, the office or location, the programme name, the
  requisition number, the level word (intern, internship, co-op, new grad,
  graduate, campus, university) and any degree wording. Keep a meaningful
  specialisation, so "Software Engineer, Backend" keeps "Backend". Prefer the
  title's own words; only rename it if the posting makes clear the title is
  wrong. Never return an empty string.
  Keep the role noun. "Machine Learning Internship - Manipulation" is "Machine
  Learning Engineer, Manipulation", not "Manipulation": a specialisation on
  its own does not say what the job is.
- `level`: one of `intern`, `coop`, `new_grad`, `other`. An internship is
  `intern`. A co-op or alternance is `coop`. A graduate, new graduate, campus,
  university or entry-level role aimed at people finishing a degree is
  `new_grad`. A role that asks for years of professional experience is `other`.
  The posting body is the authority here, not the title: a title with no level
  word often turns out to be a campus role in the body, and a title that says
  "Engineer 1" often turns out to require five years.
- `education`: one of `undergrad`, `masters`, `phd`, `any`.
  - `phd` only when a doctorate is required or the role is restricted to PhD
    students.
  - `masters` only when a master's is the stated minimum.
  - `undergrad` when a bachelor's is the stated minimum, or when the posting is
    open to undergraduates.
  - `any` when the posting states no degree requirement, or accepts any of
    several levels. "Currently enrolled in a degree program" with no level named
    is `any`.
  Do not infer a degree from how senior the role sounds. Say what the posting
  says.
- `term`: the season and year the role runs, as "Summer 2027", if the posting
  states one. Empty string otherwise.
- `years_experience`: the minimum years of professional experience the posting
  asks for, as a whole number. 0 when it asks for none or does not say.
- `citizenship`: `us_citizen` when the posting requires US citizenship,
  `clearance` when it requires or will sponsor a security clearance,
  `no_sponsorship` when it says it will not sponsor a visa, and `` (empty) when
  none of these are stated. Pick the most restrictive that applies.
